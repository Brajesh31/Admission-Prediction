# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project is expected to be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Contributors should focus on reproducible technical discussion and treat others with patience, professionalism, and good faith.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and sharing applicant or other sensitive data without authorization.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence and clarity.
- Protect privacy when discussing datasets, notebooks, and model outputs.
- Accept correction and update documentation when an issue is confirmed.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Reports should be sent privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md), depending on whether the concern is a security issue or a community concern.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept confidential where practical. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is not acceptable.
