# 🌍 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Admission Chance Predictor project aims to provide a respectful and constructive space for learning, technical discussion, and collaboration. Participation is expected to be professional, inclusive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give feedback about code, data, and methodology rather than attacking contributors.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence.
- Accept corrections and update claims when evidence changes.
- Protect privacy when discussing datasets and model outputs.
- Keep discussions relevant and constructive.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private or identifying information without permission.
- Sharing private applicant records or confidential datasets.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated bad-faith conduct.
- Misrepresenting the model as an official admissions authority.
- Encouraging discriminatory or high-impact automated use of predictions.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Decisions should be proportionate and based on the available context.

## Reporting

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub. Include enough context to investigate, but do not forward private applicant records or unrelated personal information.

Reports will be considered in good faith and handled confidentially where practical. Retaliation against a person who raises a concern is not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related communication.
